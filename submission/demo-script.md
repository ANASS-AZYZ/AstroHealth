# Demo script

1. Introduce Team EAGLES and the long-duration mission-health problem.
2. Open Guided Demo and start `Mars Crew Health Demonstration`.
3. Show live telemetry and the synchronized Digital Twin.
4. Open Mission Command Center and explain the Elevated Workload scenario.
5. Show the real configured alert in Risk Alerts.
6. Apply a response and compare before/after indicators.
7. Open Mission Intelligence for trends, comparison, and report generation.
8. Open Scientific Evidence and explain what is supported science versus simulation.
9. Close with limitations: simulated data, no clinical validation, no operational instructions.
