# Limitations

- All health measurements are simulated and are not astronaut data.
- The simulator is not a physiological, medical, or spacecraft operations model.
- Alert thresholds are configurable demonstrations, not NASA medical standards.
- Forecasts, anomaly detection, readiness scores, and response consequences are experimental software features without clinical validation.
- Radiation is represented only as an illustrative environmental dose-rate indicator; no symptoms are inferred.
- Communication delay is controlled by the simulated mission clock, not real network latency.
- Data, events, and snapshots are in-memory and are lost when the backend restarts.
- Scientific references motivate the problem framing but do not validate the AstroHealth model.
